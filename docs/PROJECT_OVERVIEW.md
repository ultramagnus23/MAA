# Project overview

## Goals

- Give Ashoka students one clear place to find the Ministry of Academic Affairs: who to contact, what the Ministry is working on, what is happening, and where the policies, guides and handbooks are.
- Make it obvious where each kind of question should go.
- Be easy for each new Ministry team to update without rewriting the site.
- Be honest. Nothing on the site is invented.

## Users

- Students looking for a policy, guide, handbook or the right person.
- Department Representatives and academic societies who want to list events or reach the Ministry.
- Ministry team members who maintain the site.

## Main journeys

| A student wants to... | They go to... |
| --- | --- |
| Ask something about their own department | Representatives, then email or the department WhatsApp group |
| Raise a general academic concern | Office Hours: book a slot, email, or WhatsApp |
| Read a policy or guide | Resources |
| Find a handbook | Handbooks |
| See what the Ministry is doing | Projects |
| Find an event | Events |
| Meet the team | About |

The home page has a "What do you need help with?" helper that sends a student to the right page.

## Scope

In scope: the pages in the sitemap, the data files that feed them, and the documentation.

Not in scope, and why:

- **Projector tracker.** No document in the project says what it should track. See [CONTENT_REGISTER.md](CONTENT_REGISTER.md).
- **Login, accounts and a database.** A login was built and then removed on the Ministry's decision. Editing happens in the repository and in a Google Sheet.
- **Booking and scheduling.** Office hours are booked on Calendly. The site links to it and does not store bookings.
- **SG report and monthly reports.** The pages and footer slot exist, but no report or link has been supplied.

## Existing and new functionality

Existing before this round: home page, resources, handbooks, representatives and the 2024–25 archive, events list (from a Google Sheet), the event instructions page, the hero video support.

New in this round: Projects with a transparency archive, the About team directory by department, the events calendar with filters and event pages, the Office Hours page, WhatsApp groups for 21 departments, the Marketing page, a footer with an SG report slot, data checks, and the full documentation set.

Removed: the separate Calendar page (merged into Events).
